# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and project discussions. Critique the work, give people room to correct mistakes, and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and disclosure of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, and restrict participation to keep project spaces usable. Report conduct concerns through a [repository issue](https://github.com/BharathKmalviya/Aggressive-Pomodoro/issues/new) with only information you are comfortable making public. Do not post private details there. For abuse on GitHub that needs a private report, use [GitHub's abuse reporting route](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Security vulnerabilities have a separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
